Ketch

A tracker firmware for the M-VAVE FM-1: song, chain and phrase screens, 4 channels on Felucca's sound engines. Version 0.1-beta (beta).

Try it in the browser
The firmware itself, in an emulator. Nothing is installed. Install on your FM-1
Over USB, in Chrome or Edge. Or go back to official V15.
